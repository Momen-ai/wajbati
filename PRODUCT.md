# Wajbati (وجباتي) — Product Overview

## What is Wajbati?
Wajbati is a premier two-sided culinary marketplace connecting food lovers with passionate independent chefs, cloud kitchens, artisan cooks, and boutique restaurants. Unlike mass-commercial aggregators, Wajbati celebrates culinary craft, authenticity, and signature handcrafted dishes ("نكهة أصيلة وشغف حقيقي"), bringing the finest handcrafted dishes and culinary stories directly to customers' tables.

## User Personas

### 1. Customers (الزبائن)
- **Profile:** Food lovers, families, students, and professionals seeking distinctive, high-quality, authentic handcrafted meals prepared with care rather than mass-produced fast food.
- **Key Actions:** Browse verified independent chefs and signature daily dishes, filter by dietary requirements and cuisine, place orders with Cash on Delivery (COD), track live order state lifecycle (`pending` → `accepted` → `preparing` → `ready_for_delivery` → `out_for_delivery` → `delivered` → `completed`), leave dish ratings and reviews upon delivery, and manage favorite dishes.

### 2. Independent Chefs & Kitchen Partners (الطهاة والمطابخ المستقلة)
- **Profile:** Talented culinary artisans, professional chefs, independent kitchen creators, and boutique restaurants looking to showcase their signature menus and scale their business flexibly.
- **Key Actions:** Apply for partner verification, manage menu items and dish availability, set daily order capacity to guarantee quality and freshness, manage real-time order preparation status transitions, export orders to CSV, and track daily/monthly net earnings.

### 3. Administrators (إدارة المنصة)
- **Profile:** Operations and support teams ensuring marketplace quality, safety, and compliance.
- **Key Actions:** Review and approve/reject chef and kitchen applications, manage meal categories, oversee marketplace user accounts, monitor system health, inspect immutable order status audit logs, and analyze platform-wide delivered revenue metrics.

## Brand Tone & Values
- **Authentic & Handcrafted:** Warm, passionate, and authentic ("نكهة أصيلة وشغف حقيقي بأيدي أمهر الطهاة").
- **Reliable & Transparent:** Clear order state transitions, verified culinary partners, transparent preparation times, and accurate pricing snapshotting.
- **Culinary Excellence & Community:** Empowering independent culinary entrepreneurs and celebrating rich food culture with dignity and mastery.
