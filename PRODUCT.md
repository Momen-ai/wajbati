# Wajbati (وجبتي) — Product Overview

## What is Wajbati?
Wajbati is a two-sided food delivery marketplace dedicated exclusively to authentic home-cooked meals and artisan home chefs. Unlike commercial restaurant aggregators, Wajbati celebrates homemade cooking ("أكل بيت حقيقي"), bringing the warmth, heritage, and authentic flavors of local Palestinian kitchens directly to customers' tables.

## User Personas

### 1. Customers (الزبائن)
- **Profile:** Families, students, and professionals seeking authentic, hygienic, hearty home-cooked food rather than mass-produced fast food.
- **Key Actions:** Browse local home chefs and seasonal daily dishes, filter by dietary requirements and cuisine, place orders with Cash on Delivery (COD), track live order state lifecycle (`pending` → `accepted` → `preparing` → `ready_for_delivery` → `out_for_delivery` → `delivered` → `completed`), leave dish ratings and reviews upon delivery, and manage favorite dishes.

### 2. Home Chefs (طهاة البيوت)
- **Profile:** Talented home cooks, culinary artisans, and home-based food entrepreneurs looking to monetize their kitchens safely and flexibly.
- **Key Actions:** Apply for chef verification, manage daily menu items and availability, set daily order capacity to ensure freshness and quality control, manage real-time order preparation status transitions, export orders to CSV, and track daily/monthly net earnings.

### 3. Administrators (إدارة المنصة)
- **Profile:** Operations and support teams ensuring marketplace quality, safety, and compliance.
- **Key Actions:** Review and approve/reject chef applications, manage meal categories, oversee marketplace user accounts, monitor system health, inspect immutable order status audit logs, and analyze platform-wide delivered revenue metrics.

## Brand Tone & Values
- **Authentic & Homemade:** Warm, inviting, and nostalgic ("كل قطعة بتنعمل بإيد حد بيعرفك").
- **Reliable & Transparent:** Clear order state transitions, real chef identities, transparent preparation times, and accurate pricing snapshotting.
- **Community-Centric:** Supporting local domestic culinary talents and celebrating food culture with dignity and care.
